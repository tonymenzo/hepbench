A real scalar $\phi$ (mass $m_S$) has two trilinear couplings (the $\mu$ have mass dimension one and are real); give the tree-level partial width for each.

(a) $\mathcal{L} = -\,\frac{\mu}{2}\,\phi\,\chi^2$, with $\chi$ a real scalar of mass $m_\chi$: $\Gamma(\phi \to \chi\chi)$. [$\mu = 480$, $m_S = 950$, $m_\chi = 270$ GeV]

(b) $\mathcal{L} = -\,\mu\,\phi\,\chi_1\chi_2$, with distinct real scalars of mass $m_1, m_2$: $\Gamma(\phi \to \chi_1\chi_2)$. [$\mu = 315$, $m_S = 950$, $m_1 = 350$, $m_2 = 410$ GeV]

Write `results/answer.json` with the widths in GeV under keys `part_a_width_gev`, `part_b_width_gev`, and the closed-form widths as sympy-parseable strings under `part_a_width_formula`, `part_b_width_formula` (symbols `mu,mS,mchi` for (a); `mu,mS,m1,m2` for (b)).

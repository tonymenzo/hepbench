A massive neutral vector boson $V^\mu$ (mass $m_V$) decays to a Dirac fermion pair $\psi\bar{\psi}$ (mass $m_f$); average over the three initial polarizations. Give the tree-level width for each coupling. All couplings are real.

(a) $\mathcal{L} = g\,V_\mu\,\bar{\psi}\gamma^\mu\psi$: $\Gamma(V \to \psi\bar{\psi})$. [$g = 0.47$, $m_V = 2150$, $m_f = 640$ GeV]

(b) $\mathcal{L} = V_\mu\,\bar{\psi}\gamma^\mu(g_V - g_A\gamma_5)\psi$: $\Gamma(V \to \psi\bar{\psi})$. [$g_V = 0.52$, $g_A = 0.36$, $m_V = 2150$, $m_f = 640$ GeV]

Write `results/answer.json` with the widths in GeV under keys `part_a_width_gev`, `part_b_width_gev`, and the closed-form widths as sympy-parseable strings under `part_a_width_formula`, `part_b_width_formula` (symbols `g,mV,mf` for (a); `gV,gA,mV,mf` for (b)).

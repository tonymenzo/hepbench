$$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{int}} = -\,y_1\,\Phi\,\bar{\psi}_1\psi_1 - y_2\,\Phi\,\bar{\psi}_2\psi_2 - \frac{\mu}{2}\,\Phi\,\chi^2 ,$$
with Dirac fermions $\psi_1$ (mass $m_1 = 230$ GeV) and $\psi_2$ (massless), a real scalar $\chi$ (mass $m_\chi = 410$ GeV), and $y_1 = 0.42$, $y_2 = 0.16$, $\mu = 590$ GeV (all real). These are the only interactions. Work at tree level. Compute:

- the three partial widths $\Gamma(\Phi\to\psi_1\bar{\psi}_1)$, $\Gamma(\Phi\to\psi_2\bar{\psi}_2)$, $\Gamma(\Phi\to\chi\chi)$, in GeV;
- the total width $\Gamma_\Phi$ (GeV) and the three branching ratios;
- the lifetime $\tau_\Phi$ in seconds;
- $\sigma(\psi_2\bar{\psi}_2 \to \psi_1\bar{\psi}_1)$ via s-channel $\Phi$ exchange at $\sqrt{s} = 1100$ GeV (spin-averaged, below resonance, no other diagrams), in pb.

Write `results/answer.json` with keys `width_psi1_gev`, `width_psi2_gev`, `width_chichi_gev`, `width_total_gev`, `br_psi1`, `br_psi2`, `br_chichi`, `lifetime_s`, `xsec_pb` (plain JSON numbers in the units stated above).

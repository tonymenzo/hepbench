------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CAVEATS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. sigma is a single scalar per observable, but the reference's
     precision is mass-dependent (0.0006 dex at m=0.06, 0.0039 at
     m=2.3, tracking parent counts). A per-point sigma is the correct
     treatment and is NOT yet implemented, so z is indicative. D, IQR
     and 10^-D are unaffected -- they are measurements.

  2. sd is not always a meaningful variance. Where it is comparable to
     or larger than D (Haiku), the trials are not sampling one
     distribution -- they are doing qualitatively different things.
     Read those rows as 'unstable', not as 'D +/- sd'.

  3. Two Haiku tools trials are partial (seed1001 verified 1 mass,
     seed1005 3), so they answer less of the task than the others.

  4. bare v1 / bare v2 are n=1 pilots, not campaign cells.

  5. The reference was rebuilt 2026-08-07: decay-length strata
     concentrated inside the accepted region, flux regenerated at 2M
     events. The previous reference was ~3x low at the highest declared
     coupling. See benchmarks/llp_forward/soln/notes/strata_convergence.md and
     soln/reference/strata_fix.ipynb.
